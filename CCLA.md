## Signing instructions

To execute this Agreement, an authorized representative of the Corporation must:

1. Contact `legal@aplexica.com` and receive secure submission instructions. Do not publish corporate Schedule A information in a public issue or pull request.
2. Provide the Corporation name, address, country, point of contact, e-mail, and the initial Schedule A of designated employees through that secure submission path.
3. Include the canonical CCLA execution block below, signed by the authorized representative, in the secure submission specified by Aplexica Legal:

   ```
   I have read and accept the Aplexica Corporate CLA on behalf of the Corporation
   identified below and am duly authorized to bind the Corporation to its terms.

   Corporation: <legal name>
   Representative: <printed name>
   Title: <title>
   Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
   ```

4. The Aplexica legal contact at `legal@aplexica.com` will counter-sign and record the Agreement once authority is verified.

Designated employees listed in the accepted Schedule A do not need to sign the Individual Contributor License Agreement separately for Contributions made within the scope of their authority on behalf of the Corporation. To amend Schedule A after execution, contact `legal@aplexica.com` and follow the secure update instructions; the most recently accepted written Schedule A is the authoritative roster.
